About

A career built around research, teaching, and explanation.

Academic depth presented through a practical, student-centered approach.

Clear thinking belongs in both the research lab and the classroom.

I picked up Computer Science at NUCES Lahore not knowing it would turn into a lifelong habit of explaining things for a living. An MS at LUMS pulled me into computational geometry under Dr. Nabil Mustafa, and a research studentship at Aarhus University, under Lars Arge, taught me to think in I/O-efficient algorithms. Somewhere along the way I realised what I actually loved wasn't just solving the problem — it was watching a student's face when they finally see it too. That's what's kept me at the front of a classroom for over twenty years, currently as a Teaching Fellow at ITU, and before that across FAST-NUCES, FOIT-UCP, and Namal College.
